Activities and Workshops

Design statement

In this module, I, with my teammate Khant Nyar Lu, looked into different physical computing and 3D design practical applications as paths to pursue for a semester-long project. We explored a variety of concepts through relevant case studies and techniques for audiovisual design in live interactive and performance settings. We aim to use this semester to take a deep dive into technical skills in TouchDesigner and explore unique ways to develop musical interactions that go beyond the conventions of using instruments and production tools to create meaningful connections. Our focus on technical skills will help lay the groundwork in developing larger-scale projects, like stage backdrops and installations, later on in our careers.

Activity 1 - Path research

I focused the two paths of audiovisual design and projection mapping that I considered to occupy my time in this course. With WGSN down and the fact that my interests are more aligned with an artistic path rather than a design one, I focused my findings looking into recent case studies that employ techniques and concepts that I want to explore further. Audiovisual design and projection mapping are fairly niche in the design world, so I thought to shift my focus from trends to researching how these technologies are being used in the entertainment industry today.

Activity 2 - Informed ideation

After Activity 1, I connected with Khant, since he was also interested in learning TouchDesigner further and pursuing audiovisual design using his own music. We decided to collaborate to help each other fill in the gaps of our collective knowledge in music production and audiovisual design in TouchDesigner.

Project Concept

Exploring musical interaction through audiovisual design

During my time in this course, I plan to use the semester to dive deeper into learning live audiovisual techniques in TouchDesigner with my teammate Khant. We will be exploring a variety of different interactions that we can later integrate into meaningful music experiences, such as live performances, installations, exhibitions, and personal projects. These interactions could include gesture controls and webcam input using the MediaPipe plugin for TouchDesigner, body and motion input with a Kinect, and instrument, MIDI, and audio input with Ableton. We aim to strengthen our technical skills and build confidence before working on larger-scale projects.

Overall Reflection

Through this introductory module, I’ve taken the opportunity to further pursue my interests in TouchDesigner and audiovisual design, as well as connect with Khant, who shared similar interests and goals in this discipline. We looked into case studies that employ concepts and techniques that we want to try ourselves, and gain insight into practical applications in the industry today. Through our analyses and dissections, we were able to learn more about the processes of developing these performances and installations. In doing our research, and importantly through feedback from our peers and professor, we came to a humbling realization taking the step back to focus our time just on learning technique rather than creating a whole installation. We saw the amount of work that goes into these projects and understood how big it might be to take this on with just the two of us. Seeing that we may be getting too ahead of ourselves, we instead slowed down to enjoy the process and take our time to dive into TouchDesigner. Overall, I’m looking forward to learning about the music production side of things, and how Khant and I can help each other fill in the gaps in our collective knowledge of audiovisual design.
